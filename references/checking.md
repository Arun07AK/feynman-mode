# Checking, and the final report

## It works

The whole syllabus is covered, topic boundaries are right, navigation works, every topic has a lecture or a documented gap, threads launch and reopen, past-paper references are valid, and progress persists.

## It teaches

Read every scope block critically, and run real threads on the hardest topics. A thread should:

- teach fully without the lecture;
- stay intuitive and accurate at the right depth;
- cover the prerequisites;
- use pictures and examples that really help;
- adapt when the learner is confused without leaving scope;
- still make sense when read cold;
- leave the learner able to explain, apply and answer exam questions.

If teaching stays abstract or incomplete, find the cause and fix the scope, the split or the order.

A polished interface or a well-written prompt is not proof of learning.

## Proportion

Hard rules: correct maths, past papers word for word, no invented questions. Everything else gets one light pass. Fix what can be fixed, and name plainly whatever is left unresolved.

## Deliverables

- the verified, structured curriculum;
- the star-rated roadmap and exam weightage;
- the index, topic views and roadmap;
- the prompt builder and a checked thread prompt for every topic;
- launch, reopen and progress;
- the lecture spine with `SPINE.md`;
- practice, mocks, saved progress and the revision layer;
- `TASKS.md`, `SCOPE.md` and the data files behind them;
- the completed functional and teaching review.

## The final report

Finish with exactly three sections.

- **Blocked on you:** decisions, payments, access or input needed. Say "none" if none.
- **Changed:** what was built and what was verified.
- **Found:** what the curriculum work, research and review turned up, including any blocking problem (where, what, how to reproduce), any topic whose teaching is still weak, lecture gaps, and anything unverified.
